## 🎥 RECORDING TIPS

### Before Recording:
1. ✅ Test your app thoroughly
2. ✅ Prepare a sample contract (PDF)
3. ✅ Clear browser cache
4. ✅ Close unnecessary tabs
5. ✅ Use 1080p screen recording
6. ✅ Test your microphone
7. ✅ Write down key points

### During Recording:
1. ✅ Speak clearly and at moderate pace
2. ✅ Use cursor/pointer to highlight features
3. ✅ Avoid long pauses
4. ✅ Show, don't just tell
5. ✅ Keep within 5-7 minutes
6. ✅ Be enthusiastic but professional

### Recording Tools (Free):
- **OBS Studio** (Windows/Mac/Linux) - Best quality
- **Loom** - Easy to use, direct upload
- **ShareX** (Windows) - Lightweight
- **QuickTime** (Mac) - Built-in
- **Windows Game Bar** (Win+G) - Built-in Windows

### After Recording:
1. ✅ Watch the entire video
2. ✅ Check audio quality
3. ✅ Verify all features shown
4. ✅ Add title card (optional)
5. ✅ Upload to YouTube/Google Drive
6. ✅ Set to Public or Unlisted
7. ✅ Copy the link for submission
